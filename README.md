# Indriya Gold Jewel

Build a premium, mobile-responsive E-commerce Product Detail Page (PDP) for Indriya (an Aditya Birla Group jewellery brand). The page is for a specific product: the "Giriraj Gold Pendant" (SKU: GDAYA00-AEPF128). The design should feel luxurious, warm, and trustworthy, utilizing a color palette of pristine white backgrounds, royal blue accents, and elegant gold typography. Ensure the layout is built using modern UI components (like Tailwind CSS) with a clean, uncrowded aesthetic.




Please structure the page with the following components:




1. Global Header & Breadcrumbs:




Header: Include the Indriya logo placeholder in the center, a hamburger menu for mobile, and icons for Search, Wishlist, and Cart on the right.

Breadcrumbs: Just below the header, include SEO-friendly breadcrumb navigation: Home > Jewellery > Gold Pendants > Giriraj Gold Pendant.

2. Main Product Section (Two-Column Layout on Desktop, Stacked on Mobile):




Left Column (Image Gallery):




A large main product image placeholder for the gold pendant, featuring a hover-to-zoom effect.

A vertical or horizontal carousel of 4 smaller thumbnail images.

Include descriptive alt text on all images for SEO (e.g., alt="Giriraj Gold Pendant in 22kt Antique Gold").

Right Column (Product Details & Buy Box):




H1 Title: "Giriraj Gold Pendant" (Elegant, serif typography).

SKU/Subtitle: "Style Code: GDAYA00-AEPF128" in smaller, muted text.

Price: Display the price prominently, accompanied by a small "View Price Breakup" link to emphasize Indriya's "transparent pricing" pillar.

Short Description: A brief, warm description highlighting the traditional craftsmanship (e.g., temple motifs or antique finish).

CTAs: Two primary, full-width buttons. A solid gold button for "Add to Cart" and an outlined royal blue button for "Book an Appointment" or "Try at Home".

3. Trust Signals Banner (Crucial for Brand):




Directly below the main product section, span a full-width, visually distinct banner (perhaps a light cream or pale blue background) featuring three trust icons with short text:




Icon 1: "BIS Hallmark Certified Gold"

Icon 2: "100% Transparent Pricing"

Icon 3: "Lifetime Exchange & Buyback"

4. Product Specifications (Accordion/Tabs Component):




Create a collapsible accordion or tabbed interface for detailed SEO-rich text.

Tab 1: Product Details: Placeholders for Gold Purity (e.g., 22KT), Weight (g), Dimensions, and Collection Name.

Tab 2: Brand Story: A short paragraph incorporating the brand tagline, "Every Jewel Tells a Story".

5. Similar Products (Cross-Selling):




A "You May Also Like" carousel section featuring 4 placeholder product cards. Each card needs an image, title, price, and a quick "Add to Wishlist" heart icon.

6. Technical SEO & Meta Tags:




In the underlying code, ensure the page has a semantic HTML5 structure. Include a placeholder for the page title (<title>Giriraj Gold Pendant | Indriya Jewellery</title>) and Product Schema markup (JSON-LD) for pricing and availability.
